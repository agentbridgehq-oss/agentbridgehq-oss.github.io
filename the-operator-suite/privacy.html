Last updated: September 5, 2026

The Operator Suite is a one-time digital product sold by Kenny Hunter (AgentBridge). This page covers how we handle information when you visit the site or buy the suite.

How we use it

To take payment, send your unlock link, answer support, and keep records required by law. We do not sell, rent, or trade your email.

Payments

Your files

After you pay, you keep the playbooks. We do not need a membership login. Bookmark your library link. Do not share paid files as free downloads.
